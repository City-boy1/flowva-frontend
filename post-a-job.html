Tips for a great Project post

1
Be specific about deliverables — file formats, dimensions, and how many revisions you expect.
2
Share reference images or brand guidelines as attachments to help creators match your vision.
3
Set a realistic budget. Browse the marketplace to see what similar work sells for.
4
A clear deadline helps creators plan. Build in buffer time for revisions.
