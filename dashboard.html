FAQs

Common questions about payments, uploads, and your account

How do payouts work?

Flowva uses an escrow system. When a buyer purchases your template, funds are held securely and released to your Skrill or Grey wallet weekly or monthly. Creators earn 80% of each sale. The first 50 creators earn 90%. Payouts are made in USD only. Skrill requires your registered Skrill email; Grey requires your Grey account number or email.

When do payouts occur?

Payouts are processed weekly or monthly depending on your selected payout frequency. Go to Payouts → Settings to choose your preference.

How do I set my template price?

You set the price when uploading your template. Prices are in USD. We recommend researching similar templates on the marketplace to price competitively.

How long does template review take?

Our admin team reviews all templates before they go live. This typically takes 24–48 hours. You'll receive an email once your template is approved or if changes are needed.

How do I add my payout details?

Go to Dashboard → Payouts and enter your Skrill email or Grey account details. Payouts are made in USD only.

What file types are accepted?

Flowva accepts files from both desktop and mobile creators. Desktop: PSD, PSB, AI, EPS, SVG, INDD, CDR, Affinity, Sketch, FIG, Procreate, Krita (max 500MB). Motion graphics: AEP, PRPROJ, DRP, FCPXML, MOGRT (max 1GB). Animation: BLEND, C4D, MA, MOHO, FLA (max 1.5GB). Mobile: PLP, IBIS, ALIGHT/ALM, KMPROJECT, VNPROJ, CapCut Template Links (max 250–500MB). Universal exports: PNG, JPG, PDF, MP4, MOV (max 200MB).

Community Guidelines

Rules for creators and buyers on Flowva

Professionalism

All creators and buyers are expected to communicate respectfully. Harassment, hate speech, or discriminatory language will result in immediate account suspension.

Originality

All uploaded templates must be your own original work. Uploading plagiarised, stolen, or unlicensed content is strictly prohibited and will result in permanent removal.

Anti-Fraud Policy

Any attempt to manipulate ratings, fake purchases, or circumvent Flowva's escrow system is a serious violation. Fraudulent accounts will be permanently banned and reported.

Respectful Communication

All messages between buyers and creators must remain professional. Disputes should be raised through the official dispute system, not through personal messages.
