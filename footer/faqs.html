Payments & Escrow

Flowva uses an escrow system. When a buyer purchases a template or hires a creator, funds are held securely by Flowva and only released to the creator once the buyer confirms satisfaction. This protects both parties throughout every transaction.

Buyers can pay using Skrill, Grey, Mastercard, or Bank Account. Flowva does not use Paystack or cryptocurrency payments. Escrow secures the payment but does not process it directly.

Creators receive payouts in USD only via Skrill or Grey. Skrill payouts go to your registered Skrill email address. Grey payouts go to your Grey account number or email. Add your payout details in Dashboard → Payouts → Payout Settings.

Payouts are processed weekly or monthly depending on your selected payout frequency. You can set your preference in Dashboard → Payouts → Settings. All payouts are made in USD only.

Creators keep 80% of every sale. The first 50 creators on Flowva earn a special 90% commission rate as a founding reward. Flowva retains 20% (or 10% for early adopters) as a platform fee.

Escrow & Safety

When a buyer makes a payment, funds are held in Flowva's escrow — a secure holding layer — rather than going directly to the creator. Once the transaction is confirmed and any dispute window closes, escrow releases the creator's share. Escrow protects both parties from fraud and ensures creators are paid fairly.
