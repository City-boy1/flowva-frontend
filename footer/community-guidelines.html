Introduction

Flowva is a trusted digital marketplace connecting designers, motion graphics artists, animators, and buyers from over 60 countries. Our platform is built to empower creators to earn fairly while giving buyers access to high-quality creative assets.

These guidelines apply to every user — whether you are uploading a template, purchasing one, posting a job, hiring a freelancer, or participating in the Flowva community chat. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of these guidelines.

Creator Conduct

Creators are the backbone of Flowva. We expect a high standard of professionalism, quality, and honesty in everything you publish and every interaction you have on the platform.

Upload Original Work

All templates must be your own original creation. Work created using tools such as Photoshop, After Effects, Canva, Figma, Alight Motion, and others must reflect your genuine skill and effort.

Accurate Descriptions

Titles, categories, previews, and descriptions must honestly represent your template. Misleading listings will be removed and may result in account suspension.

Deliver on Commitments

If you accept a project or freelance job, you are expected to deliver within agreed timelines, communicate proactively, and handle revisions professionally.

Proper Licensing

Any third-party assets (fonts, stock elements, plugins) included in a template must be properly licensed for commercial resale. Disclose all asset sources in your listing description.

No Stolen Assets

Uploading templates that were made by another creator without permission — or passing off purchased bundles as your own — is strictly prohibited and will result in permanent removal.

No Fake Reviews

Soliciting, purchasing, or otherwise manipulating your own ratings is prohibited. Flowva actively monitors for review manipulation and will take enforcement action.

Buyer Conduct

Buyers are valued members of the Flowva community. When purchasing templates, posting projects, or hiring freelancers, you are expected to act in good faith and treat creators with respect.

  • Pay for every template you download. Attempting to share, resell, or redistribute purchased files is a violation of your license agreement.
  • Provide clear, realistic briefs when posting projects. Vague or misleading briefs that bait creators are against our policy.
  • Do not request free samples or unpaid work as a condition of a job post or project brief.
  • Raise disputes through the Flowva support system only — not through chargebacks or payment reversals without first contacting support.
  • Leave honest, constructive reviews. Reviews must reflect your genuine experience and may not be used as leverage against creators.

Originality & Intellectual Property

Flowva takes intellectual property seriously. All uploaded work must be original and must not infringe on any trademarks, copyrights, or other proprietary rights.

  • Do not upload templates that incorporate trademarked logos, brand assets, or copyrighted characters without written authorization from the rights holder.
  • Do not copy or closely replicate templates from other creators and list them as your own original work.
  • Stock assets included in your template must be licensed for commercial redistribution. Extended or editorial licenses may be required.
  • If you believe your intellectual property has been infringed, contact us at flowva3@gmail.com with evidence and we will investigate promptly.

Anti-Fraud Policy

Flowva uses an escrow-based payment system to protect both buyers and creators. Any attempt to circumvent this system, conduct off-platform transactions, or manipulate the escrow release process is a serious violation.

No Off-Platform Payments

Requesting or accepting payment outside of Flowva's escrow system is strictly prohibited and voids all dispute protections for both parties.

No Chargeback Abuse

Filing fraudulent chargebacks for digital goods that were delivered as described is a violation of our policy and will result in permanent account termination.

No Account Sharing

Each Flowva account must be owned and operated by one person. Sharing credentials or selling accounts is prohibited.

No Multiple Accounts

Creating multiple accounts to circumvent bans, inflate download counts, or receive multiple free-tier benefits is not allowed.

Respectful Communication

All communication through Flowva's messaging system — between creators and buyers, or in the community chat — must remain professional and respectful.

  • No harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language directed at any user.
  • No unsolicited promotional messages, spam, or affiliate links sent through the messaging system.
  • Do not attempt to redirect buyers to external websites or off-platform tools to conduct transactions.
  • All files shared through messages must be safe, virus-free, and relevant to the project at hand.

Community Chat

Flowva provides a dedicated community group chat for creators to connect, share knowledge, seek feedback, and collaborate. This space is a privilege, not a right.

  • Keep discussions relevant to design, animation, templates, and creative work.
  • Constructive criticism is welcome; personal attacks are not.
  • Do not flood the chat with excessive self-promotion. One post per new template is reasonable.
  • Moderators have the authority to remove messages, mute users, or ban members who repeatedly violate community standards.

Prohibited Content

The following types of content are strictly prohibited on Flowva under any circumstances:

Adult or Explicit Content

Templates, previews, or messages containing pornographic or sexually explicit material.

Hate Speech

Content that promotes discrimination, hatred, or violence based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, or nationality.

Malicious Files

Uploading templates that contain malware, spyware, ransomware, or any code designed to harm the user's device or data.

Illegal Content

Any content that violates applicable law in the creator's or buyer's jurisdiction, including but not limited to DMCA violations.

Enforcement

Flowva reserves the right to take enforcement action in proportion to the severity and frequency of guideline violations. The following table outlines our general enforcement approach:

Violation Level Examples Action
Minor Incomplete listing description, minor communication issue Warning notice via email
Moderate Misleading template description, off-platform payment request Listing removal + 7-day suspension
Serious IP theft, fake reviews, chargeback abuse Account suspension pending investigation
Severe Malicious files, hate speech, fraud, repeated violations Permanent account termination and escrow forfeiture

Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action was taken in error, you may submit an appeal within 14 days of notification. Send your appeal to flowva3@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Appeal — [Your Username]" and include a clear explanation and any supporting evidence.

Flowva will review all appeals fairly and respond within 5–7 business days. Decisions on appeals are final.
