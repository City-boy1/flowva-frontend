Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions our team hears most often.

Creators receive 80% of every sale. Flowva retains 20% as a platform commission. The first 50 creators to join the platform earn an elevated rate of 90%. All payouts are made in USD only.
Payouts are processed on a weekly or monthly schedule depending on your preference — configurable in your Dashboard under Settings → Payout. Once released from escrow, funds typically settle to your Skrill or Grey wallet within approximately 2 minutes.
Flowva supports Skrill and Grey only. For Skrill, you connect using your registered Skrill email address. For Grey, you connect your Grey account details. Both are set up in your Dashboard under Settings → Payout. No other wallets or payment methods are supported at this time.
Flowva supports sellers from a range of African, European, American, Asia-Pacific, and Middle Eastern countries via Skrill. African countries include Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Egypt, Ethiopia, and more. Outside Africa, supported regions include the UK, USA, Germany, France, India, UAE, and others. If your country is not listed during signup, it is currently unsupported. All payouts are in USD only.
Yes. Buyers can purchase templates using Skrill, Grey, Mastercard, or Bank Account. All payments are routed through Flowva's escrow system before funds are released to the creator. There are no crypto payment options on Flowva.
Flowva accepts over 60 file formats across six categories: Graphic Design (PSD, AI, EPS, SVG, FIG, SKETCH, CDR, Affinity, Procreate, and more), Mobile Design (PLP, IBIS, PicsArt PXD, CapCut links), Motion Graphics (AEP, MOGRT, PRPROJ, DRP, FCPXML, and more), Animation (BLEND, C4D, MA, FLA, SPINE, and more), Mobile Animation (ALIGHT, KMPROJECT, VNPROJ), and 3D Assets (OBJ, FBX, GLB, GLTF, STL). Universal export formats like PNG, JPG, PDF, MP4, and MOV are also supported.
Size limits vary by file category on the free tier: Desktop design files (PSD, AI, Figma, etc.) up to 500MB; Mobile design files up to 250MB; Motion graphics files up to 1GB; Animation files up to 1.5GB; Mobile animation files up to 500MB; 3D assets up to 1GB; Universal exports up to 200MB. Pro and Enterprise plans allow significantly higher limits and total storage quotas.
Absolutely. Flowva fully supports mobile creators. If your software — such as Alight Motion, Canva, CapCut, Kinemaster, IbisPaint, or Pixellab — can generate a template file, you can upload it on Flowva. Mobile creators are equal members of the marketplace.
The actual template file (PSD, AEP, etc.) is stored securely in the cloud and only accessible to buyers after purchase. On the marketplace, buyers see a PNG preview image you upload when listing your template. You will also provide a download link that activates upon purchase.
There are three tiers. The Free tier is automatic for all new creators — no credit card needed, limits appear only when uploading. The Pro plan costs $19/month or $190/year, and includes up to 50GB total storage, custom storefront branding, and advanced analytics. The Enterprise plan costs $99/month or $990/year and includes 500GB+ storage, API bulk uploads, white-label storefronts, a dedicated account manager, and premium CDN delivery.

After successful signup you will receive a pop-up notification prompting you to verify your email. Check your inbox for a message from Flowva with a verification link and onboarding instructions. You will not be able to upload templates or receive payouts until your email is verified.
When a buyer makes a payment, the funds are held in Flowva's escrow system — a secure holding layer — rather than going directly to the creator. Once the transaction is confirmed and any dispute window closes, escrow releases the creator's share automatically. Escrow is a security layer, not a payment processor. It protects both parties from fraud and ensures creators are paid fairly.
All disputes must be raised through Flowva's support system — not through your bank or payment provider. Contact us at flowva3@gmail.com or use the contact form on this page. Filing a chargeback directly without first contacting Flowva support is a violation of our policy and may result in permanent account termination.
No. Requesting or accepting payment outside of Flowva's escrow system is strictly prohibited and voids all dispute protections for both the buyer and the creator. All transactions — including freelance work and project jobs — must go through Flowva's platform.
Because templates are digital downloads, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. If the template was not delivered as described, or if there was a technical issue, contact flowva3@gmail.com with your order details. Do not initiate a chargeback without first contacting Flowva support.

The Flowva community group chat is available to all registered creators. Once your account is set up and verified, look for the community join button on the homepage or in your dashboard. The community is a space for creators to connect, share knowledge, seek feedback, and collaborate.
