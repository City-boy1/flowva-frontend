What We Believe

A marketplace built on trust

Flowva exists to give creative professionals — from mobile designers to 3D motion artists — a fair, secure place to sell their work and grow their income globally.

For Every Creator

Whether you design on a phone or a workstation, if your software generates a template, you can sell it on Flowva. Mobile and desktop creators are equally welcome.

Escrow-Secured Payments

Every transaction is protected through Flowva's escrow system. Funds are held securely and released to creators only after buyer confirmation — no risk for either side.

Real Earnings

Creators keep 80% of every sale — or 90% if they're among our first 50 creators. Payouts are made in USD via Skrill or Grey, weekly or monthly.

Global Reach

Flowva supports buyers and creators across Africa, Europe, the Americas, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East — with payment options that work locally.

How It Works

Simple for creators. Simple for buyers.

Flowva removes the friction from buying and selling creative assets — so you can focus on the work.

1

Sign Up

Create your account in minutes. Buyers can browse immediately. Creators apply for a creator account and get reviewed within 48 hours.

2

Upload or Browse

Creators upload templates — from PSD files to After Effects projects to mobile formats like Alight Motion. Buyers browse by category, software, or creator.

3

Buy Securely

Buyers pay via Skrill, Grey, Mastercard, or Bank Account. Funds go into escrow instantly — protecting both parties throughout the transaction.

4

Download & Earn

Buyers download immediately after payment is confirmed. Creators receive their earnings — 80% of every sale — paid out in USD on their chosen schedule.
