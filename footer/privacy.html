Overview

Flowva ("we", "us", "our") is a digital marketplace that connects graphic designers, motion graphics artists, animators, and buyers worldwide. When you use Flowva — whether as a creator uploading templates or a buyer purchasing them — you entrust us with personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, the purposes for which we use it, how it is stored securely, and what rights you have over your data. It applies to all users of the Flowva platform, including our website, dashboard, and community tools.

Flowva does not sell your personal data to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Your data is used exclusively to operate and improve the Flowva platform.

Data We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on how you interact with Flowva:

Category Data Points When Collected
Account Data Name, email address, username, country, profile image, bio On signup and profile setup
Payout Data Skrill email address or Grey wallet account details When added in Dashboard → Settings → Payout
Transaction Data Purchase history, sale records, escrow status, commission amounts On each transaction
Content Data Uploaded templates (stored in cloud), preview images, listing metadata When you upload templates
Usage Data Pages visited, features used, session duration, device type Automatically during platform use

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Account management: To create and manage your account, verify your email, and authenticate logins.
  • Platform functionality: To display your templates, process purchases through our escrow system, and facilitate buyer–creator transactions.
  • Payouts: To process your earnings and release funds to your Skrill or Grey wallet on your selected schedule (weekly or monthly).
  • Communications: To send transactional emails (account verification, purchase confirmations, payout notifications) and platform announcements.
  • Safety and fraud prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent activity, guideline violations, and abuse of the escrow system.
  • Platform improvement: To understand how users interact with Flowva and to develop better features and user experiences.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Payment processors (Skrill, Grey): Your payout wallet details are shared only with your chosen payment provider to release your earnings. These providers have their own privacy policies and comply with applicable financial regulations.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers: Template files are stored with third-party cloud storage services under strict data processing agreements. Only preview images (PNG) are publicly displayed on the platform.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business transfers: If Flowva is acquired or merges with another entity, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified via email before such a transfer occurs.

Payment & Escrow Data

All purchases on Flowva are processed through our escrow system. We do not store full card numbers or bank account details directly. Payment processing is handled through our integrated gateways (Skrill, Grey, Mastercard, and Bank Account transfers).

For creator payouts, your Skrill email address or Grey account details are stored securely in your Dashboard under Settings → Payout. This information is encrypted at rest and is never displayed in plain text in any admin interface.

Transaction records — including the amount paid, commission retained by Flowva (20%), the creator's net payout (80%), and the payout date — are retained for a minimum of 7 years to comply with financial record-keeping requirements.

Storage & Security

Flowva takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS). Sensitive data at rest (payout details, personal identifiers) is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Template files (actual design files) are stored in cloud infrastructure separate from the public web servers. Only the PNG preview image is publicly accessible.
  • We perform regular security audits and vulnerability assessments on our platform infrastructure.
  • In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you via email within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

Cookies

Flowva uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate the platform and improve your experience. We use the following categories of cookies:

Cookie Type Purpose Can Be Disabled?
Strictly Necessary Session management, authentication, CSRF protection No — required for the platform to function
Functional Remembering your preferences (e.g., light/dark mode, currency) Yes, via browser settings

We do not use advertising cookies or share cookie data with advertisers. Flowva is and will remain an ad-free platform.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data via your dashboard settings.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to legal retention requirements.

Right to Object

Object to certain types of processing, such as analytics tracking.

Right to Portability

Receive a machine-readable export of your data (account info, transaction history).

Right to Restrict

Request that we limit how we process your data while an issue is being investigated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at flowva3@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Flowva is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that a minor has registered on our platform, we will promptly delete their account and all associated data.

If you believe that a minor has created an account on Flowva, please notify us immediately at flowva3@gmail.com.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or the features we offer. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
  • Send a notification email to all registered users.
  • Display an in-platform banner for 30 days following the change.

Continued use of Flowva after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
